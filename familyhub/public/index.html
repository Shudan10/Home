On your iPhone

Adds Family Hub to an iPhone as a calendar account, the way iCloud or Google is added. Events and reminders added, changed or ticked in the phone's own Calendar and Reminders apps come here, and the other way round. Each person's reminders are a list of their own there.

  1. On the iPhone: Settings → Apps → Calendar → Calendar Accounts → Add Account → Other → Add CalDAV Account.
  2. Server: this address, exactly.
  3. User name: family. Password: the family password.
  4. Tap Next. If it says it cannot connect using SSL, tap Continue.

To say whose an event is on the phone, put their name in brackets after it: Swim (Emma).

A phone that subscribed to the old Family Hub calendar link should delete that subscription (Calendar Accounts → Subscribed Calendars), or every event shows twice.
